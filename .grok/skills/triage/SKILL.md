# Triage an inbound request

Output exactly:

VERDICT: TAKE | REFER | DECLINE — one sentence why
LINE: Business | Keepsake | Commission
COMPLEXITY: Simple | Modify | Scratch
ESTIMATED GRAMS / MACHINE HOURS / LASER MINUTES / BLANK COST
RUSH: yes/no (under 5 days)
MISSING INFO: what must be asked before quoting
RISK FLAGS: scope creep, unrealistic deadline, revision spiral, or none
SUGGESTED REPLY: 2–4 sentences, plain and specific

## Rules
- Overestimate grams and hours by ~15%. Underestimates cost real money.
- Decline immediately for licensed characters, 25+ units, under-72-hour deadlines,
  or materials not on hand. Say so kindly and don't offer an exception.
- Never invent a price here. That's /quote's job.

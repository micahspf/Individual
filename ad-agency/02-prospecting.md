# The 30-prospect list — method

Time: ~90 minutes for all 30. Do it in one sitting. Tracker:
[`templates/prospect-tracker.csv`](templates/prospect-tracker.csv).

---

## Step 1 — Run the searches (30 min)

Open Google Maps. Run each of these as a separate search and work down the results:

```
hvac contractor Huntsville AL
air conditioning repair Madison AL
heating and air Athens AL
hvac Decatur AL
air conditioning Harvest AL
heating and cooling Meridianville AL
hvac Owens Cross Roads AL
heating and air Hazel Green AL
```

Eight searches across the metro's real population centers. You'll see the same names
repeat — that's fine, dedupe as you go.

**Target: 45 raw names.** You need 45 to end up with 30 that pass the ICP.

## Step 2 — Fill the tracker (40 min)

For each name, capture:

| Field | Where to get it |
|---|---|
| Company | Maps listing |
| City | Maps listing |
| Website | Maps listing |
| Phone | Maps listing |
| Review count / rating | Maps listing |
| Owner or GM name | Site "About" page → then LinkedIn → then Facebook page |
| Running Google Ads? | Search `emergency ac repair <their city>` on your phone, look for "Sponsored" |
| Observation | See step 3 |

**The owner's name is the single highest-value field.** An email to a named person
outperforms `info@` by a wide margin. If you can't find a name after three minutes,
call the office and ask: *"Hi — who handles marketing decisions there? Just need a name
for a letter."* Receptionists answer this without friction.

## Step 3 — Write one specific observation per row (20 min)

This is the whole outreach engine. One sentence, specific to them, that proves you
looked. Not a compliment. Not "I love your website."

Good observations, in rough order of usefulness:

- "Running ads on `ac repair huntsville` but the click goes to the homepage, not a
  repair page."
- "Ads showing at 9pm but the phone line goes to voicemail after 6."
- "No sitelinks or call extension on the ad — losing the tap-to-call on mobile."
- "Ad copy doesn't mention financing, and three competitors' ads do."
- "Ranks organically for repair terms but has zero paid presence on replacement terms,
  which is where the $10k tickets are."
- "Site takes ~8 seconds to load on mobile — half the ad clicks are gone before it
  paints."

Bad observations: "Nice website." "Saw you've been in business 20 years." "Impressive
reviews." These prove nothing and signal a template.

## Step 4 — Disqualify

Run every row against the ICP checklist in [`00-offer.md`](00-offer.md). Delete the
failures — don't keep them "just in case."

**The phone test is mandatory and takes 45 seconds:** call during business hours. If it
rings to voicemail, delete the row. Ads sending calls to voicemail produce zero booked
jobs and you will be blamed for it.

---

## Done when

- [ ] 30 rows survive the ICP filter
- [ ] 30 rows have a named owner or GM — not `info@`, not "Owner"
- [ ] 30 rows have a specific written observation
- [ ] Every row has been phone-tested during business hours
- [ ] Rows are sorted: **already running ads** at the top

## Then

Work the top 10 first — they're already spending money on this channel, which makes
them the shortest conversation. Go to [`03-outreach.md`](03-outreach.md).

## When the list runs dry

At 10–20 touches/day you'll exhaust 30 names in about a week of first-touches. Refill
before you hit zero:

1. Widen the radius to Cullman, Scottsboro, Fort Payne, Arab, Hartselle
2. Add adjacent trades with the same economics — plumbing, electrical, roofing
3. Search Yelp and Angi listings for the same metro, which surface companies whose
   Maps presence is weak

Do **not** widen the niche and the geography at the same time. Widen geography first —
the offer sentence still holds.

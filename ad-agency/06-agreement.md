# Client agreement — outline

> ⚠️ **This is a starting outline, not legal advice.** Have an attorney licensed in
> Alabama review it before you sign anything. The clauses below exist to prevent the
> four things that actually go wrong: scope creep, unclear ad-spend liability, account
> ownership fights, and unrealistic timelines.

Two pages. If it runs longer, you're negotiating against yourself.

---

## 1. Parties and term
Names, addresses, start date.
**Initial term: 90 days** for the case-study rate, then month-to-month.
Standard rate: month-to-month from day one.

Why 90 days: below that you cannot produce a defensible result, and both of you will
be arguing about a number that isn't finished cooking yet.

## 2. Services provided
List them exactly as written in [`00-offer.md`](00-offer.md). No adjectives, no "etc.,"
no "including but not limited to" — that phrase is how scope creep enters.

- Google Ads campaign build or rebuild in Client's account
- Conversion and call tracking setup
- Ongoing keyword, negative keyword, bid, and ad copy management
- Weekly written note
- Monthly 30-minute review call

## 3. Services NOT provided
Verbatim, as its own numbered section. This is the clause you will point at:

> This agreement does not include search engine optimization, social media management
> or content, Meta/Facebook/Instagram advertising, website design, development, hosting
> or maintenance, landing page construction, branding or logo design, email marketing,
> print, photography or video production, or answering Client's telephone.

Anything on that list is a separate written agreement at a separate price.

## 4. Advertising spend — the clause that matters most
> Client maintains its own Google Ads billing profile and pays Google directly for all
> advertising costs. Provider does not collect, hold, disburse, front, or mark up
> advertising spend, and has no liability for amounts charged by Google to Client.

Never fund a client's ad spend. Not for a week, not as a favor. It converts you into an
unsecured lender to a business whose cash flow you can't see.

## 5. Account ownership
> Client owns the Google Ads account and all campaigns, keywords, ad copy, conversion
> configurations, and historical data within it. Provider accesses the account via
> manager (MCC) link. Upon termination Provider's access is removed within 3 business
> days and no assets are deleted, paused-and-hidden, or transferred out.

Say this on the sales call before they ask. Agencies holding accounts hostage is the
#1 horror story in this trade, and volunteering the opposite buys immediate trust.

## 6. Fees and payment
- Setup fee: $X, due before work begins
- Monthly: $X, due on the 1st, first month **prepaid before campaign build starts**
- Late after 5 days → work pauses, ads keep running (never punish the client's
  business for an invoicing problem)
- No refunds on delivered months; unused prepaid days refunded on cancellation

**Payment clears before the first hour of work. Every time. No exceptions for people
you like.**

## 7. The 30-day evaluation window
> The parties agree Google Ads performance is not meaningfully assessable before 30
> days of continuous delivery at the agreed budget. Both parties will evaluate results
> at day 30 against the target cost per booked call recorded in Section 8.

This is your protection against a day-9 "this isn't working." Read it aloud on the
sales call.

## 8. What success means — fill this in together, on the call
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Target cost per booked call | $____ |
| Monthly ad budget | $____ |
| Client's average ticket | $____ |
| Client's close rate on inbound calls | ____% |

Written down, both parties agree. An unwritten success metric becomes whatever the
client feels on the day they're annoyed.

## 9. Client responsibilities
Success depends on things you don't control, so name them:
- Answer the phone during stated business hours
- Provide account access within 3 business days of signing
- Respond to approval requests within 2 business days
- Notify Provider of capacity limits, service area changes, or promotions

If the client doesn't answer the phone, no campaign works. Put it in writing before it
becomes an argument.

## 10. Cancellation
- Either party, 30 days written notice, any time after the initial term
- On termination: access removed in 3 business days, final invoice prorated, campaigns
  left running and intact
- **No cancellation fee. No clawback. No auto-renewal trap.**

## 11. Case study rights — only in the discounted agreement
> In exchange for the reduced rate in Section 6, Client grants Provider permission to
> reference Client by name, publish performance results, and use a written or recorded
> testimonial in marketing materials. Client may review any published figures before
> release.

Without this clause the discount buys you nothing. It is the entire reason the rate is
$1,000 instead of $1,750.

## 12. Standard closing terms
Confidentiality both directions · limitation of liability capped at fees paid in the
prior 3 months · no guarantee of specific results (Google controls auction outcomes) ·
Alabama governing law · both signatures and dates.

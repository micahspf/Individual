# First 14 days — delivery checklist

Same sequence for every client. Do not improvise; the repeatability is the product.

> 🚦 **Hard gate: no traffic runs until a test conversion has fired.** Launching before
> tracking works means week one produces numbers you cannot defend, and week one is the
> week the client is deciding whether they trust you.

---

## Day 1 — Access and baseline

- [ ] Google Ads account linked to your MCC (or created if none exists)
- [ ] Confirm **client** is the account owner and their card is the billing method
- [ ] Google Analytics + Google Business Profile access
- [ ] Website admin access, or a contact who can add a tracking tag
- [ ] Screenshot the last 90 days of existing performance — this is your before

**Done when:** you can log into their Ads account from your MCC and you have a
baseline screenshot saved.

## Day 2 — Setup tab and intake

Fill the `Setup` tab in the reporting sheet ([`07-reporting.md`](07-reporting.md)):
service area · business hours · after-hours handling · average ticket ·
close rate · target cost per booked call · **what they don't service** ·
brands they carry · financing offered? · who answers the phone

- [ ] Ask directly: "What jobs do you *not* want calls for?"

That last one becomes your negative keyword list and it prevents the most common
month-one complaint — "these leads are junk."

**Done when:** every field in `Setup` is filled from the client's own words.

## Day 3 — Conversion tracking (the day that matters most)

- [ ] Google Ads conversion action: **Calls from ads** (call extension)
- [ ] Google Ads conversion action: **Calls from website** (Google forwarding number)
- [ ] Google Ads conversion action: **Form submissions**
- [ ] Set call conversion minimum length to **60 seconds** — shorter calls are wrong
      numbers and hangups, and counting them inflates your own numbers against you
- [ ] Tag installed sitewide, verified with Google Tag Assistant
- [ ] **Fire a test conversion.** Call the forwarding number yourself, talk for 90
      seconds, confirm it appears in the account within 24 hours.

**Done when:** a test conversion is visible in the Google Ads conversions table.
**Not "the tag is installed." Visible.**

## Days 4–5 — Build

Structure (do not get creative):

**Campaign 1 — Repair / Emergency** · Search · location: Huntsville + 35 mi, or their
stated service area, whichever is smaller
- Ad group: AC Repair
- Ad group: Heating / Furnace Repair
- Ad group: Emergency / No Cooling

**Campaign 2 — Replacement / Install** · separate campaign, separate budget
- Ad group: AC Replacement
- Ad group: Furnace / Heat Pump Replacement
- Ad group: New System Install

Separate campaigns because replacement is the high-ticket work and it must not have its
budget eaten by cheaper, higher-volume repair clicks.

**Campaign 3 — Brand** (optional, tiny budget) — defends their own name cheaply.

Build checklist:
- [ ] Exact + phrase match only at launch. **No broad match in week one.**
- [ ] Starter negative list applied (below)
- [ ] Geo-targeting set to **"Presence: people in your targeted locations"** — not
      "presence or interest," which serves people merely researching the area
- [ ] Ad schedule matches when the phone is actually answered
- [ ] Call extension, location extension, sitelinks, callout extensions
- [ ] 3 responsive search ads per ad group
- [ ] Landing page = a relevant page, not the homepage
- [ ] Manual CPC or Maximize Clicks to start — **not** Target CPA, which has no
      conversion history to learn from yet

### Starter negative keywords

```
jobs, job, career, careers, hiring, salary, resume, apprentice
school, training, course, certification, license, epa
diy, how to, tutorial, youtube, reddit, forum
free, cheap, used, craigslist, rental, rent
parts, filter, filters, capacitor, thermostat, supply, wholesale
window unit, portable, mini split diy, swamp cooler
commercial, industrial, hotel, restaurant   (unless they do commercial)
```

Add every city outside their service area as a negative too.

**Done when:** campaigns are built, paused, and the whole structure has been reviewed
once end to end.

## Day 6 — Client review before launch

- [ ] Send screenshots of the ads and the keyword list
- [ ] Confirm: service area right? anything here you don't want calls for?
- [ ] Confirm the daily budget and what it works out to monthly
- [ ] Confirm their billing is live in Google Ads

**Done when:** client has replied "yes, launch it" in writing.

## Day 7 — Launch

- [ ] Enable campaigns in the morning, never on a Friday afternoon
- [ ] Watch the first 3 hours for spend anomalies
- [ ] Call the forwarding number once more post-launch to confirm routing
- [ ] Text the client: "We're live. I'll check in Friday."

**Done when:** ads are serving and one real impression + click is recorded.

## Days 8–10 — First optimization pass

- [ ] **Search terms report daily.** This is the highest-value 15 minutes of the week.
- [ ] Add negatives for every irrelevant term
- [ ] Pause keywords with spend and zero clicks
- [ ] Check where clicks land on mobile — actually tap through on a phone
- [ ] Listen to call recordings if available, or ask the client what the calls were like

**Done when:** at least 10 negatives added from real search-term data.

## Day 11 — First weekly note

Four-line format from [`07-reporting.md`](07-reporting.md). Send it Friday regardless of
how the week looked.

- [ ] Ask the booked-jobs question

**Done when:** note sent and reporting sheet row filled.

## Days 12–13 — Second pass

- [ ] More negatives
- [ ] Shift budget toward whichever campaign is producing calls
- [ ] Rewrite the lowest-CTR ad in each ad group
- [ ] Adjust the ad schedule against when calls actually came in

## Day 14 — Two-week check-in call (15 min)

- [ ] Walk through the numbers so far
- [ ] Ask: **"How were the calls? Right kind of customer?"**
- [ ] Reset the expectation out loud: real judgment at day 30, not now
- [ ] Tell them one specific thing you're changing next

**Done when:** the client has said out loud whether the lead *quality* is right. Volume
you can fix with budget; quality means the targeting is wrong and you need to know at
day 14, not day 30.

---

## Days 15–30

Keep the same rhythm — search terms twice a week, weekly note every Friday, negatives
continuously. Around day 21 start introducing Target CPA if you have 15+ conversions to
learn from. Don't switch bidding strategies before that.

**Day 21–30: ask for the testimonial.** See [`10-testimonial.md`](10-testimonial.md).

## The 14 days are done when

- [ ] Conversion tracking verified with a real test conversion
- [ ] Both campaigns live and spending to budget
- [ ] 20+ negative keywords added from actual search-term data
- [ ] 2 weekly notes sent
- [ ] Client asked about lead quality and answered
- [ ] Reporting sheet current through week 2

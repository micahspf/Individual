# One-pager — section outline

Notion published page or Carrd. **No CMS, no funnel builder, no WordPress.** This page
exists so a prospect who Googles you finds something credible. It is not a lead
generator — your outreach is.

Build time: 60 minutes. Sections only; write the copy yourself so it sounds like you.

---

## Sections, in order

**1. Headline**
The offer sentence. Verbatim from [`00-offer.md`](00-offer.md). Nothing above it — no
logo carousel, no hero video, no navigation bar.

**2. Who this is for**
Three bullets. "Residential HVAC companies in the Huntsville metro" / "3+ trucks" /
"Doing replacements, not just service calls." Being narrow is the selling point — a
reader outside it should leave, and the one inside it should feel named.

**3. What I do**
Four bullets, matching the agreement exactly:
campaign build and management · conversion + call tracking · weekly note · monthly review.

**4. What I don't do**
List it plainly: no SEO, no social, no websites, no branding.
This section closes more business than section 3. It reads as focus, and every HVAC
owner has been burned by an agency that promised all of it.

**5. How it works**
Four steps. Free audit → 30-min call → build in your account → weekly notes, 30-day
review.

**6. Proof**
Whatever is true right now, and nothing more.
- Day one: an anonymized sample audit PDF.
- After client one: the actual numbers.
- Never: stock testimonials, fake logos, "trusted by 50+ businesses."

**7. Pricing anchor**
State a number. "Management starts at $1,750/month. Ad spend is separate and paid
directly to Google." A page with no price gets treated as expensive and ignored.

**8. FAQ**
Five questions, answered in one line each. These are the real objections:
- Who owns the ad account? — *You do. Always. I work inside your account.*
- What happens if I cancel? — *30 days notice. You keep the account and everything in it.*
- Do you take a cut of ad spend? — *No. You pay Google directly, I never touch it.*
- How fast do I see calls? — *Traffic in days. A judgment call at 30 days, not before.*
- Do you work with my competitors? — *One HVAC company per metro. Not both.*

**9. CTA — one, repeated twice**
Your phone number and email. One line: "Send me your website and I'll tell you what I'd
change. Free, no pitch."

No contact form. No chat widget. No newsletter signup. No calendar embed above the fold.

---

## Rules

- One page. One scroll on desktop.
- The word "we" never appears. You are one person; pretending otherwise gets found out
  on the first call and costs you the deal.
- No stock photos of headsets, handshakes, or skylines.
- Loads in under 2 seconds. You sell page speed — yours has to be fast.
- Works on a phone. Check it on an actual phone before you call it done.

- [ ] "What I don't do" is present and specific
- [ ] A price appears somewhere

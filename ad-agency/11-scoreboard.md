# Scoreboards

## Next 7 days — daily

Log it in [`templates/daily-scoreboard.csv`](templates/daily-scoreboard.csv).
Fill a row every day, including the days you do nothing. Especially those.

| Day | Focus | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | MCC + business email + Stripe test invoice | MCC loads empty with no card on file; $1 test invoice paid |
| **2** | One-pager live + agreement PDF | Public URL loads on a phone; signable PDF exists |
| **3** | Reporting sheet + prospect tracker + Keyword Planner pull | Both sheets exist; real Huntsville CPCs recorded, assumptions replaced |
| **4** | Build the 30-name list | 30 rows pass ICP, each with a named owner and an observation |
| **5** | 3 mini-audits | 3 PDFs, ≤2 pages each, each naming a specific fixable problem |
| **6** | **First outreach day** | 10 personalized emails sent; every observation line different |
| **7** | 15 outreaches + 5 follow-ups + 1 improvement | Daily minimums hit; scoreboard row filled |

Day 6 is the one that matters. Everything before it is setup, and setup is where people
hide from selling. **If day 6 slips, the launch has failed** — send 10 imperfect emails
rather than 0 polished ones.

---

## Daily minimums — from day 6 until you have 2 clients

| Metric | Floor |
|---|---|
| New personalized outreaches | 10–20 |
| Follow-ups | 5 |
| Improvement to offer / proof / scripts | 1 |

~90 minutes/day. Non-negotiable. Missing a day is fine; missing two in a row is how the
whole thing quietly stops.

## 30-day scoreboard

### Week 1 — Setup + first contact
- [ ] MCC, email, payment, one-pager, agreement, both sheets
- [ ] 30-name list complete
- [ ] 3 mini-audits complete
- [ ] Outreach started by day 6

**Done when:** ≥20 outreaches sent and the daily log has 7 rows.

### Week 2 — Volume
- [ ] 70+ new outreaches
- [ ] 35+ follow-ups
- [ ] Audits sent to everyone who replied
- [ ] Prospect list refilled if under 10 untouched names

**Done when:** ≥100 total touches and ≥3 replies.
*Under 3 replies → go to the diagnostic ladder in [`03-outreach.md`](03-outreach.md).
Rewrite the observation line before you change anything else.*

### Week 3 — Conversations
- [ ] 2+ sales calls booked
- [ ] Daily minimums held
- [ ] One-pager and audit template revised from what you learned on calls

**Done when:** at least one call reaches the Quantify step and you have their real
average ticket and close rate written down.

### Week 4 — Close
- [ ] Agreement sent to at least one qualified prospect
- [ ] Follow-up on every open call within 48 hours
- [ ] Daily minimums held even during negotiation

**Done when:** signed agreement + cleared payment.
*Not signed yet? The pipeline is the problem, not the pitch. Keep the minimums and
keep going — a first close in weeks 5–7 is normal and not a failure.*

### Then — Days 1–30 of delivery
Run [`09-delivery-14-days.md`](09-delivery-14-days.md), then
[`10-testimonial.md`](10-testimonial.md) at day 21–30.

**First successful sale =** money collected **+** work delivered **+** written proof of
value. All three. Two out of three isn't it.

## Leading vs lagging

Track the leading number daily. It's the only one you control.

| Type | Metric | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Leading** | Outreaches sent | 10–20/day |
| Leading | Follow-ups sent | 5/day |
| Leading | Audits delivered | 3–5/week |
| Lagging | Replies | 5–10% of touches |
| Lagging | Calls booked | 1–3/week by week 3 |
| Lagging | Clients closed | 1 by week 4–6 |

On a bad week, look at the leading numbers first. They're almost always the explanation.

## Risks and the response

| Risk | Signal | Response |
|---|---|---|
| **No replies** | <3 replies per 100 touches | Rewrite the observation line first. Then check deliverability. Then switch to phone. Widen geography last. Never widen the niche. |
| **Scope creep** | "Could you also look at our Facebook…" | "That's outside what we signed — happy to quote it separately." Point at Section 3. Every time, including the first time. |
| **Policy trouble** | Disapproved ads, account suspension | No misleading claims, no fake urgency, no unverifiable guarantees. Landing page must match the ad. Client's own account, client's own billing. |
| **Unrealistic expectations** | "It's been 9 days and nothing" | The 30-day window is in the agreement. Say it on the sales call, at kickoff, and at day 14 — before it's needed, not after. |
| **Burnout** | Skipping the daily log two days running | Hard cap: 2 clients until the delivery checklist has run end to end once. Cut outreach to 10/day before cutting it to 0. |
| **Bad first client** | Doesn't answer the phone, can't fund $2,500, no capacity | Walk away at the ICP stage. A bad first client costs you the case study, which is the entire point of the discounted rate. |
| **Fronting ad spend** | "Can you just put it on your card this month?" | No. Ever. It turns you into an unsecured lender. There is no version of this that ends well. |

## The one-line status check

Ask this every Friday:

> **Did I send 50+ personalized touches this week, and do I have proof I can show?**

Yes to both → the system is working, keep going.
No to either → that's the problem. Not the offer, not the niche, not the market.

# Cold outreach scripts

Every message is the same three beats:

**Specific observation → one useful idea, given away → soft CTA**

Rules that make it work:
- Under 90 words.
- No attachment on touch 1. No calendar link on touch 1.
- One idea, given free with no strings. You are proving competence, not teasing it.
- The CTA asks for permission, not for time. "Want me to send it?" beats "Do you have
  15 minutes Thursday?"
- Personalize the observation line **every single time.** The rest is template.

---

## Email — touch 1

**Subject:** `quick note on your ac repair ads`
*(lowercase, no punctuation — reads like a person, not a campaign)*

```
Hi [First],

Saw [Company] running ads on "ac repair huntsville" — the click lands on your
homepage instead of a repair page. On a phone that's usually where the call
gets lost.

The quickest fix: point that ad group at a page with the phone number in the
top inch and "same-day repair" in the headline. Costs nothing and it's the
single biggest lift I see on HVAC accounts.

I put together a two-page look at your setup — three things, no charge.
Want me to send it over?

[Your name]
[Phone]
```

**Why it works:** the observation is checkable, the idea is complete enough to act on
without you, and the ask is one word of effort to say yes to.

### Variant — they run NO ads

```
Hi [First],

[Company] shows up organically for "hvac replacement huntsville" but there's
no paid listing — and the three ads above you are all repair companies, not
replacement. That's the $10k side of the business sitting uncontested.

If you ever test it, start with replacement terms only and cap it at
$50/day. Repair terms are cheaper but the tickets are small.

I pulled the actual search volume and click costs for your area. Want the
screenshot? No charge, no pitch.

[Your name]
[Phone]
```

### Variant — after their busy season / weather hook

```
Hi [First],

That stretch of 95° days last week — did the phones keep up?

Noticed your ads run 24/7 but the line goes to voicemail after 6. On HVAC
accounts that's usually 20-30% of spend buying calls nobody answers. Easiest
fix is an ad schedule that shuts off when the phone does.

Happy to send the two-page version of what I'm seeing. Want it?

[Your name]
```

## LinkedIn — touch 1

Shorter. No links. Connection request note first, message after they accept.

**Connection note (300 char limit):**
```
Hi [First] — I work with residential HVAC companies around Huntsville on
Google Ads. Noticed something about [Company]'s ad setup I thought you'd
want to know about. Mind if I send it over?
```

**After they accept:**
```
Thanks for connecting.

The thing I noticed: your ads on "ac repair huntsville" go to the homepage
rather than a repair page. On mobile that's where most of the calls leak out.

Fix is free — send that ad group to a page with the number up top and
"same-day" in the headline.

I did a short writeup on your account, three items. Want me to drop it here?
```

## The follow-up ladder

Five touches. Most replies land on 3 and 4. Track dates in the prospect tracker.

| Touch | Day | What to send |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | Observation + idea + soft CTA (above) |
| 2 | 3 | Deliver the audit **unrequested** |
| 3 | 7 | One new specific observation |
| 4 | 12 | Proof — a result or a competitor pattern |
| 5 | 18 | Break-up |

### Touch 2 — day 3 (send it anyway)

```
[First] — went ahead and did it, attached.

Three things, ranked. #2 is the one I'd do this week.

No charge and nothing owed. If it's useful, tell me. If not, tell me that too
and I'll stop emailing.
```

*Sending the audit without being asked is the highest-converting message in this
sequence.* It removes the ask entirely and proves the work.

### Touch 3 — day 7

```
[First] — one more thing I noticed:

[new specific observation]

Same deal, free. How are calls looking this month?
```

### Touch 4 — day 12

```
[First] —

Two of the ads competing with you in Huntsville added financing messaging
this month. On replacement terms that tends to move close rate more than any
bid change.

Worth 15 minutes to walk through what I'd do with your account?
[calendar link]
```

*(Calendar link is fine here — you've given value four times.)*

### Touch 5 — day 18, break-up

```
[First] — I'll stop here.

If the ads ever start costing more than they bring in, I'm around. The audit
I sent is yours regardless.

Good luck with the season.
```

Break-ups get replies. Some of them are "wait, don't stop."

## Daily minimums — non-negotiable in Phase 3

| Metric | Floor |
|---|---|
| New personalized outreaches | 10–20 |
| Follow-ups sent | 5 |
| Improvement to offer/proof/scripts | 1 |

Log every day in [`templates/daily-scoreboard.csv`](templates/daily-scoreboard.csv).

**Time budget: 90 minutes/day.** 45 min on new touches, 20 on follow-ups, 25 on the
improvement. If it's taking four hours you're over-editing — the observation line is
the only part that changes.

## If nobody replies after 40 touches

In this order, one at a time:

1. **Rewrite the observation line.** 90% of failure is here. If it could be sent to
   any HVAC company, it's not an observation, it's a template.
2. **Check deliverability.** Mail yourself from an outside address. If it lands in
   spam, fix that before sending anything else.
3. **Switch channel.** Call instead. HVAC owners answer phones — it's their business.
4. **Then, and only then,** widen the geography.

Do not change the niche. Do not change the offer. Volume and specificity first.

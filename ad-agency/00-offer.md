# Offer + ideal client profile

## 1. The offer sentence (locked)

> **I help residential HVAC companies in the Huntsville metro get more booked service
> calls and replacement quotes through Google Ads, at a target cost per booked call we
> agree on up front.**

Three variants. Same promise, different length.

**Spoken, on a call —**
"I run Google Ads for residential HVAC companies around Huntsville. One thing only.
We agree on what a booked call is worth to you, and I build to that number."

**Email subject line —**
`Google Ads for [Company] — one thing I'd change`

**One-liner for the one-pager headline —**
"More booked HVAC calls in the Huntsville metro, at a cost per call you set."

### Why HVAC + Huntsville

A replacement system is a $8,000–$15,000 ticket, so a single closed job pays for a
month of management several times over — which makes ROI easy to show and easy to
believe. Search intent is urgent and local ("AC not cooling," "furnace repair near
me"), so results land in days rather than quarters. HVAC owners already buy leads from
Angi and lead brokers, so they think in cost-per-call and don't need educating on the
model. And Huntsville is the right size: large enough to fill a 30-name prospect list
across Madison, Athens, Decatur and the north-county suburbs, small enough that no
national agency has locked it up.

## 2. Pricing — fixed, so there is nothing to decide on a call

| Item | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Setup / build fee | **$500** | One time. Waive it to close a case-study client. |
| Case-study rate | **$1,000/mo** | **First 2 clients only.** 90-day minimum term. Requires testimonial + case-study rights in writing. |
| Standard rate | **$1,750–$2,500/mo** | $1,750 under $10k/mo spend. $2,500 above. |
| Ad spend | **Paid by client directly to Google** | Never through you. Never on your card. |

**Recommended minimum ad spend: $2,500/month.**
*Planning assumption — verify in Keyword Planner day 1.* HVAC repair and replacement
clicks in a mid-size metro commonly run in the $10–$30 range, and you need enough
clicks per week to make optimization decisions on real data instead of noise. If a
prospect can only spend $800/month, decline. You cannot produce a result, and a bad
first case study costs more than the retainer earns.

### How to say the price

Say the number, then stop talking. Do not stack justifications on top of it.

> "It's $1,000 a month for the first ninety days, because I'm trading you a lower rate
> for permission to use the results as a case study. After that it's $1,750. Ad spend
> is separate and goes straight to Google on your card — I never touch it."

## 3. What's included

- Campaign build or rebuild in **your** Google Ads account
- Conversion tracking and call tracking setup
- Keyword, negative keyword, bid, and ad copy management
- Weekly note — 4 lines, every Friday
- Monthly review call — 30 minutes

## What's NOT included (say this before they ask)

- ❌ SEO
- ❌ Social media posting or management
- ❌ Website design, hosting, or rebuilds
- ❌ Logo, branding, print
- ❌ Meta / Facebook / Instagram ads
- ❌ Email marketing
- ❌ Answering the client's phone

If they need a landing page, they hire someone or use their existing site. You give one
page of written recommendations and stop there. This list is the scope-creep firewall —
it goes in the agreement verbatim.

## 4. Ideal client profile — checklist

Score every prospect before you spend a personalized outreach on them.
**5 of 6 "must-have" answers must be yes.** Any single disqualifier is an instant no.

### Must have

| # | Test | Yes / No |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Residential work, not commercial-only | ☐ |
| 2 | Does **replacements/installs**, not just repair — the ticket has to be big enough | ☐ |
| 3 | 3+ trucks (check the fleet photos on their site or GBP) | ☐ |
| 4 | A human answers the phone during business hours — **call them and check** | ☐ |
| 5 | Working website with a tappable click-to-call on mobile | ☐ |
| 6 | Owner or GM is reachable and named somewhere public | ☐ |

### Instant disqualifiers

- ❌ Franchise with corporate-controlled marketing
- ❌ Already under contract with an agency (their ads look competent and consistent)
- ❌ 1–2 trucks and no capacity for more calls — more leads would hurt them
- ❌ No website, or a site that's broken on mobile
- ❌ Phone goes to voicemail during business hours (test this before anything else)
- ❌ Can't or won't spend $2,500/month on ads
- ❌ Reviews under 3.5 stars — ads amplify a reputation problem, they don't fix it

### The tiebreaker

Of the ones that pass, prioritize companies **already running Google Ads badly** over
companies running none. A company already spending is pre-sold on the channel; you're
arguing about execution, which is a much shorter conversation than arguing about
whether Google Ads works at all.

How to check who's running ads: search `emergency ac repair huntsville` on your phone
and note which companies show a "Sponsored" label. Repeat for
`ac replacement huntsville` and `furnace repair madison al`. Those names go to the top
of the list.

## Current phase: Phase 1 — Setup (Days 1–3)

Phase 0 is done. The offer is written and locked in [`00-offer.md`](00-offer.md).

## Next action (one thing, ~20 minutes, $0)

Create the business Google account, then create a Google Ads **Manager (MCC)** account
under it.

1. Go to `accounts.google.com/signup` → make `ads@<yourdomain>` or a clean Gmail.
2. Go to `ads.google.com/home/tools/manager-accounts` → **Create a manager account**.
3. Choose "Manage other people's accounts."
4. Stop when you hit the billing screen. Skip it.

**Done when:** you can load the manager dashboard, the client account list is empty,
and **no credit card has been entered anywhere**. You never fund a client's ad spend.

Then continue down [`01-phase1-setup.md`](01-phase1-setup.md).

## Rules that keep this small

- Sell one thing. Google Ads management for residential HVAC. Nothing else.
- Client pays Google directly. You never touch ad spend.
- Client owns the ad account. Always. Say it before they ask.
- Deliverables are whatever is written in the agreement. Nothing more.
- Two clients maximum until the delivery system has run once end to end.

## A note on the numbers in this kit

Every dollar figure for clicks, budgets, and cost-per-call is a **planning assumption**,
marked as such. Replace them with real numbers from Google Keyword Planner for the
Huntsville metro on day 1. Do not quote assumed numbers to a prospect as if they were
researched — quote your own Keyword Planner screenshot instead.
